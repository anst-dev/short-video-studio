# TREATMENT — Bản 4: Hệ Lưới Tuyệt Đối (Swiss Motion Graphics)

Style: `swiss-motion` (`styles/swiss-motion/STYLE.md`)
Duration: 60.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 120 BPM (30 bars)
Language: Tiếng Việt (voiceover OmniVoice + editorial grid-integrated captions + `.srt`)
Palette: Warm Zurich page `#EAE9E5`, crisp artifact paper `#FFFFFF`, pure black ink `#111111`, light architectural grey `#D2D0C8` / `#8C8A82`, **ONE Signal Red `#E30613` protagonist** (zero gradients, zero drop shadows, zero bounce — pure `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1)` snaps & linear cuts!)

## 1. Three candidate structures & choice

1. **24-Hour Timetable Poster**: A vertical 24-row schedule of an office day filling row by row. *Static vertically, misses the multi-model PCTPR transformation.*
2. **A–Z Specimen Sheet**: 26 typographic modules from A to Z. *Artificial for a workflow narrative.*
3. **Editorial Spread + Knife-Cut Deletion + Architectural Programme (CHOSEN)**: Frame acts as a 12-column Zurich editorial spread (left page = rule/numeral/manifesto, right page = live modular poster artifact). Disorder opens as 28 unsnapped, colliding off-grid black & grey paper slips (`OFF-GRID // CHAOS`), frozen by a giant flush-left typographic Spread for Question 1 & Question 2. The 4-bar Broken Loop builds heavy black blocks (`Prompt ngắn → Kết quả sáo rỗng → Sửa tay liên tục → Mất thời gian`) until at 16.5 s a razor-sharp **Signal Red `#E30613` Knife-Cut** slices horizontally through the mess and **Re-flows** the artifact into a 4-quadrant Swiss programme (`Bảng tính động · Dashboard KPI · Checklist · Luồng dữ liệu`). Hard-pan by one spread at 25.0 s into the **5-column `PCTPR` specimen** driving `ChatGPT`, `Claude`, `Gemini`, and `GPTs/Gems`. At 38.5 s, **Extreme Scale Contrast** pits optical giant numeral `100` against `40` while a diagonal red Knife-Cut slices away 60 of 100 black grid bars; 3 crisp rule-topped museum captions verify the outputs; everything clears into generous Zurich white space (`Không gian gọn gàng · Giá trị cao`) before the giant Signal Red `50–60%` poster and final System Prompt → QA manifesto.

## 2. Logline & Arc

- **Logline**: Trên mặt giấy phẳng chuẩn phong cách đồ họa Thụy Sĩ Quốc tế với lưới 12 cột và duy nhất một sắc đỏ tín hiệu `#E30613`, sự hỗn loạn văn phòng bị cắt phăng bằng những nhát cắt hình học chính xác trên từng phách nhạc để tái cấu trúc thành hệ thống PCTPR giảm 50–60% giấy tờ.
- **Arc**: Unsnapped off-grid collision & Broken Loop blocks (0–16.0 s) → Silence + Signal-Red Knife-Cut & Swiss Grid Programme `PCTPR` (16.0–38.5 s) → Optical `100 → 40` scale contrast, 3 museum rules, generous editorial white space & `50–60%` poster finale (38.5–60.0 s).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Reference works**: (1) Karl Gerstner *Designing Programmes* & Josef Müller-Brockmann grid systems (strict 12-column flush-left ragged-right `Archivo` typography, optical alignment, heavy horizontal rules); (2) Experimental Jetset / Pentagram kinetic identity systems (snaps strictly on `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1)` without bounce, clip-rect baseline reveals).
- **What to learn**: Every arrival lands on a beat with zero overshoot; subtitles live inside the editorial column layout with a `1 px` rule above rather than a generic floating pill.
- **What not to take**: No Helvetica/Akzidenz files, no concentric-circle poster copies, no institution names.
