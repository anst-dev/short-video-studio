# TREATMENT — Bản 1: Quy Trình Ánh Sáng (Dark Tech Keynote)

Style: `dark-keynote` (`styles/dark-keynote/STYLE.md`)
Duration: 60.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 120 BPM (30 bars of 4/4)
Language: Tiếng Việt (voiceover OmniVoice + burned-in UI toast captions + `.srt`)
Brand accent: Luminous Lime `#B7F34A` (problem noise colour: `#FF4D4D`, banished permanently after 16.5 s)

## 1. Three candidate structures & choice

1. **Zoom Ladder (Pixel → Team)**: Start locked on a blinking red `#REF!` cell, pull back bar by bar through a single report, a desktop, and a whole drowning department, then snap everything into one workflow console. *Strong opening, but spends too long in abstract scale before showing the flawed prompt loop.*
2. **Split-Screen Race**: Left half runs "Short Prompt + Manual Edits", right half runs "PCTPR Pipeline" simultaneously from 0 s to 60 s. *Clear comparison, but violates the user's narrative progression from initial chaos & two hook questions into the flawed loop and then the PCTPR reveal.*
3. **Accumulation → Freeze → Snap Grid → Put-Away (CHOSEN)**: Open in media res with generated UI accumulation (reports, `#REF!` sheets, 18-page meeting docs, unread mail/chat badges) tilting in 2.5D space; freeze mid-air for the two provocative questions; dive into a close-up of the 4-step broken prompt loop; cut to digital silence at 15.8 s; at 16.5 s a living lime cursor triggers **The Snap Grid**, routing chaotic tiles along curved trails into a 4-module live system and the 5-node PCTPR multi-AI pipeline, scrambling `100%` down to `40%`, ticking through the 3 acceptance proofs, and dismantling paperwork beat-by-beat (**Put-Away**) to leave calm strategic space for the `50–60%` verdict and CTA. *Chosen because every beat of the user's story maps onto a signature `dark-keynote` native move.*

## 2. Logline & Arc

- **Logline**: Một team văn phòng thoát khỏi cơn bão báo cáo và biên bản họp hỗn loạn bằng cách thay thói quen gõ prompt ngắn bằng hệ thống PCTPR đa mô hình có kiểm chứng, cắt giảm 50–60% thời gian giấy tờ.
- **Arc**:
  - **Setup (0.0–16.2 s)**: Quá tải giấy tờ số (báo cáo, bảng tính lỗi, biên bản họp 18 trang, 47 email/chat) + hai câu hỏi thức tỉnh + vòng lặp sai lầm `prompt ngắn → kết quả sáo rỗng → sửa tay liên tục → mất thời gian`.
  - **Turn (16.2–38.5 s)**: Khoảnh khắc tĩnh lặng tuyệt đối (16.0–16.5 s), con trỏ Lime `#B7F34A` kích hoạt **Snap Grid** gom toàn bộ mảnh vỡ vào hệ thống bảng tính động, KPI dashboard, checklist & luồng dữ liệu, mở ra kiến trúc **PCTPR** phối hợp ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini và GPTs/Gems.
  - **Ending (38.5–60.0 s)**: Con số khổng lồ giảm từ `100%` xuống `40%`, 3 hạng mục nghiệm thu tự động bật xanh, các thẻ giấy tờ thu gọn dần (**Put-Away**) trả lại không gian tĩnh tại cho công việc giá trị cao, khép lại bằng con số thực tế `50–60%` và lời mời hành động.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Reference works**: (1) Linear *Method / Cycles* launch film (snap-grid dark UI precision, hairline 48 px grid, single luminous accent); (2) Apple M-series keynote stat reveals (light-sweep reveal, lone giant `Inter Tight` numeral on pure dark stage).
- **What to learn**: Generous negative space, 1 px inner highlights on dark surfaces, curved flight trails sorted by type, rhythmic phasing mallets that clear out for silence before the key reveal.
- **What not to take**: No macOS traffic lights, docks, Linear/Apple logos, brand names, or copied layouts.

## 5. Beat Sheet & Cue Map (120 BPM · 0.5 s/beat · 2.0 s/bar)

- **Bars 1–2 (0.0–4.0 s)**: 16th-note detuned glass/file plops as 28 chaotic office files accumulate; Beat 2 (0.6 s) VO `c00` enters; Question 1 headline lit at 0.8 s.
- **Bars 3–4 (4.0–8.5 s)**: Beat 11 (5.0 s) UI snap to Question 2; VO `c01` at 5.2 s.
- **Bars 5–8 (8.5–16.0 s)**: 4-beat steps (`8.5, 10.2, 11.9, 13.6 s`) lock each node of the broken loop (`Prompt ngắn` → `Kết quả sáo rỗng` → `Sửa tay liên tục` → `Mất thời gian`); VO `c02` at 8.8 s.
- **Silence #1 (15.8–16.5 s)**: Digital zero on music & ambience; screen frozen on red overload.
- **Bars 9–13 (16.5–25.0 s)**: Downbeat `16.5 s` felt mallet + sub drop + **Snap Grid** shimmer; marimba & vibraphone phasing pattern enters in D Dorian / A minor-add9 bright mode; VO `c03` at 16.8 s.
- **Bars 14–19 (25.0–38.5 s)**: Additive mallet voices as `PCTPR` pills lock (`25.2–26.0 s`), `ChatGPT` (`26.5 s`), `Claude` (`28.8 s`), `Gemini` (`31.4 s`), `GPTs/Gems` (`34.0 s`); VO `c04` (25.5 s) & `c05` (31.2 s).
- **Bars 20–24 (38.5–48.5 s)**: Beat `38.5 s` zoom to giant `100%` scrambling to `40%` (digit grain ticks on 16ths, locking on `41.5 s` with glockenspiel octave); VO `c06` (38.8 s); checklist ticks at `43.5, 45.0, 46.5 s` with VO `c07` (43.4 s).
- **Bars 25–27 (48.5–53.4 s)**: Subtractive music process — notes thin out to warm bowed vibraphone pad as paperwork retracts (**Put-Away**); VO `c08` (48.8 s).
- **Silence #2 (53.4–54.0 s)**: Clean break to −60 dB room tone.
- **Bars 28–30 (54.0–60.0 s)**: `54.0 s` luminous chord + light-sweep on `50–60%` (VO `c09`); `57.0 s` crisp cursor tick + final CTA line (VO `c10`); final chord rings cleanly to `59.8 s`.
