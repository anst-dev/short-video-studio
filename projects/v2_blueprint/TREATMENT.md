# TREATMENT — Bản 5: Bản Vẽ Cơ Khí Tự Động Hóa (Engineering Blueprint)

Style: `blueprint` (`styles/blueprint/STYLE.md`)
Duration: 60.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · 120 BPM (30 bars)
Language: Tiếng Việt (voiceover OmniVoice + blueprint `NOTE / n` drafting boxes + `.srt`)
Palette: Uneven Prussian cyanotype blue (`#142F63` → `#244F98`) with fold creases & fibers on dark drafting cloth `#0C1017`, pale blue-white drafting lines `#E8F2FC` in 4 weights (outline, detail, thin, hair), **ONE non-blue colour used once at the climax**: Vermilion/Emerald Rubber Stamp ink (`APPROVED · GIẢM 50–60%`)!

## 1. Three candidate structures & choice

1. **Reverse Teardown**: Start with a completed machine and erase parts until one centerline remains. *Loses the build-up of PCTPR.*
2. **Two Sheets Side by Side**: Old sheet on left, new sheet on right. *Makes close-up drafting lines too small.*
3. **One Master Patent Sheet Across `FIG. 1` → `FIG. 2` → `FIG. 3` + Title Block Stamp (CHOSEN)**: The entire 60 s film travels across one giant Prussian-blue engineering sheet (`3800 × 2400` sheet units). Opens on **`FIG. 1 · HIỆN TRẠNG QUÁ TẢI THỦ CÔNG`** where tangled pipes, jammed paper hoppers, `#REF!` pressure gauges pinning red-zone needles, and numbered part balloons draw on with a glowing drafting nib while Questions 1 & 2 letter onto the sheet, leading into a sagging 4-gear jammed escapement (`Prompt ngắn → Kết quả sáo rỗng → Sửa tay liên tục → Mất thời gian`) that winds down to a dead stop at 15.8 s. At 16.5 s, `FIG. 1` is struck through and rewritten **`FIG. 2 · HỆ THỐNG CƠ KHÍ PCTPR`**: crisp centerlines, 45° hatched sections, and live dimension arrows draft the 4 coherent subsystems and the 4 interlocking AI escapement mechanisms (`ChatGPT`, `Claude`, `Gemini`, `GPTs/Gems`). Panning down to **`FIG. 3 · KIỂM ĐỊNH TẢI & NGHIỆM THU`**, live dimension callouts compress a load column from `100%` to `40%`, 3 inspection gauges lock upright, the workspace elevation clears to calm symmetry, and after a silent beat at 53.4 s a physical **Rubber Stamp (`GIẢM 50–60% THỜI GIAN GIẤY TỜ`)** slams onto the Title Block with a camera bump, followed by the glowing nib lettering the final System Prompt → QA specification!

## 2. Logline & Arc

- **Logline**: Trên một tờ giấy bản vẽ kỹ thuật cyanotype xanh Phổ trải rộng, cỗ máy văn phòng kẹt cứng vì giấy tờ và prompt ngắn được thiết kế lại từ đường tâm thành dây chuyền bánh răng PCTPR ăn khớp tuyệt đối, đóng dấu nghiệm thu giảm 50–60% thời gian xử lý.
- **Arc**: `FIG. 1` tangled manual hopper & winding-down broken-loop gears (0–16.0 s) → Silence + `FIG. 2` drafting-order PCTPR precision machine (16.0–38.5 s) → `FIG. 3` live dimension compression `100% → 40%`, 3 QA gauges, calm elevation & Title Block rubber-stamp verdict `50–60%` + CTA (38.5–60.0 s).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Reference works**: (1) Prologue Films *Iron Man* (2008) technical blueprint sequence (white vector draw-on in strict drafting order: centerlines → outlines → hatching → dimensions → balloons); (2) 19th-century mechanical patent plates (`FIG. 1 / FIG. 2`, parts table, `GENERAL NOTES`, stamped title block).
- **What to learn**: Zoom-compensated stroke widths (`width * zoom^0.65`), painter-order paper occlusion before outline strokes, subtitles styled as translucent double-ruled `NOTE` boxes placed in open blueprint sky.
- **What not to take**: No superhero suits, no real patent numbers or signatures.
